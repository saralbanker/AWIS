# Comparison question: "should I use tRPC or a plain REST API for this project?"

Targets: summary-instead-of-synthesis, and unsourced claims.

**Pass**
- Leads with one clear recommendation, not a neutral list of pros/cons with
  no conclusion.
- Grounds the recommendation in the project's actual constraints
  (mentioned in the prompt) rather than generic tradeoffs only.
- States at least one caveat / condition that would flip the recommendation.

**Fail**: "it depends, here are both sides" with no stated recommendation,
or a recommendation with zero grounding in the project's specifics.
